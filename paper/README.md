# Accompanying survey

**From Pixels to Knowledge: A Survey of Document Parsing, Understanding, and Agentic Reasoning**

- Author: Jia Luo
- Affiliation: Huazhong University of Science and Technology
- Target venue: Artificial Intelligence Review
- Scope: document parsing, document understanding, VLM-based analysis, efficient document intelligence, and agentic/world-model extensions.

The public repository contains the survey figures, a curated reading path, the manuscript bibliography, benchmark notes, and a forward-looking document-world-model design guide. The submission source and submission metadata are intentionally kept outside the public repository package.
